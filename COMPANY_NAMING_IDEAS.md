## 🔍 Name Evaluation Criteria

**Great Company Names Should Be:**
- ✅ **Memorable** - Easy to remember and recall
- ✅ **Professional** - Suitable for enterprise sales
- ✅ **Scalable** - Works for future products beyond transcription
- ✅ **Brandable** - Can build a strong brand around it
- ✅ **Available** - Domain and social handles available
- ✅ **Pronounceable** - Easy to say in meetings and presentations
- ✅ **Meaningful** - Suggests what the company does

## 🌐 Domain & Brand Considerations

**Check Availability For:**
- `.com` domain (most important)
- `.ai` domain (tech-forward alternative)
- Social media handles (Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Trademark conflicts
- Similar company names in your space

**Brand Extension Potential:**
- Can the name work for future AI products?
- Does it limit you to just transcription?
- Could it work for other business software?
- Is it broad enough for company growth?
