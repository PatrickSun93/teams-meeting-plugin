## 📝 App Store Listing Information

### Basic Information

**App Name:** Teams Meeting Transcription with AI Summary

**Short Description (80 characters max):**
Real-time meeting transcription and AI-powered summaries for Teams meetings

**Full Description (4000 characters max):**

Transform your Microsoft Teams meetings with intelligent, real-time transcription and AI-powered summaries. This comprehensive solution captures every word, identifies speakers, and generates actionable meeting summaries automatically.

**Key Features:**

🎤 **Real-Time Transcription**
- Instant speech-to-text conversion during meetings
- Support for multiple languages and accents
- High accuracy with confidence indicators
- Works with both local and cloud STT services

👥 **Speaker Identification**
- Automatic speaker recognition and labeling
- Handles multiple participants and overlapping speech
- Learns and adapts to voice patterns
- Clear speaker attribution in transcripts

🤖 **AI-Powered Summaries**
- Intelligent meeting summaries focused on agenda items
- Automatic action item and decision extraction
- Custom prompt support for personalized summaries
- Integration with OpenAI, Claude, and Azure AI services

💬 **Seamless Teams Integration**
- Automatic transcript delivery to meeting chat
- Host-controlled transcription with participant visibility
- Meeting agenda integration for context-aware summaries
- No disruption to normal Teams functionality

🔒 **Privacy & Security First**
- Local processing option for sensitive meetings
- Encrypted storage for all transcripts and data
- Configurable data retention policies
- User consent flows for cloud service usage

⚙️ **Flexible Configuration**
- Choose between local models or cloud services (OpenAI, Claude, Azure)
- Custom summary prompts for different meeting types
- User-specific preferences and API key management
- Multiple STT service options for optimal accuracy

**Perfect For:**
- Business meetings and conference calls
- Training sessions and workshops
- Client presentations and demos
- Team standups and retrospectives
- Board meetings and executive sessions

**Supported Services:**
- Local STT: Whisper.js and browser-native recognition
- Cloud STT: OpenAI Whisper, Azure Speech Services, Claude
- AI Summaries: GPT-4, Claude, Azure OpenAI
- Meeting Platforms: Microsoft Teams (primary), with extensible architecture

**Getting Started:**
1. Install the app from Teams App Store
2. Configure your preferred transcription and AI services
3. Join a Teams meeting and activate transcription (hosts only)
4. Receive automatic transcripts and summaries in meeting chat

Experience the future of meeting productivity with intelligent transcription that keeps your team focused on what matters most.
